My Story


Hi, I'm Anjali, a B.Tech student currently pursuing Computer Science and Engineering with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML).


Honestly, coding wasn't something I always imagined myself doing. 
There was a time when I thought this field probably wasn't meant for me.
But things changed as I explored new subjects and discovered interests 
I never expected to have.

My interest in mathematics started growing around my 9th and 10th grades,
which encouraged me to continue studying it in classes 11 and 12.
Later, I started preparing for JEE, although I eventually stepped away
from that preparation. Interestingly, near the end of my 12th grade,
I made another unexpected decision: I switched from Hindi to Computer Science 
as a subject just 3 months before from the board exams .

I started preparing for Computer Science almost from scratch, and somewhere
along the way, I discovered a genuine interest in it. What once felt 
unfamiliar slowly became something I wanted to explore further.
And now, here I am, building my own website and learning things I never imagined I would.

This journey has taught me something important: never decide that something isn't 
meant for you before giving yourself a chance to try it.You don't have to know 
everything from the beginning, and you don't need to have your 
entire future figured out. Sometimes, all you need is curiosity, 
patience, and the willingness to put in the effort.

This website is one small part of my journey—a reminder to myself that growth often begins 
when we step outside what feels familiar. I'm still learning, exploring, and figuring things 
out, and that's exactly what makes this journey exciting.There's always something new to 
learn, something new to try, and another version of ourselves waiting to be discovered.
